Your Tennessee Therapist List — 3 Steps, No Tech Skills Needed

This builds two Excel files: every speech therapist and every occupational therapist in Tennessee — names, credentials, practice addresses, and phone numbers — from the government's public provider registry.

1
Put the two files RUN_ME_Therapist_List.bat and tn_therapist_list.ps1 in the same folder (your Desktop is fine).
2
Double-click RUN_ME_Therapist_List.bat. A black window opens and starts counting through zip codes — that's it working. Leave it alone for 5–10 minutes.
If Windows shows a blue "protected your PC" box: click More info, then Run anyway. (It's your own file — Windows just doesn't recognize it.)
3
When it says Done, look on your Desktop for:
📄 TN_Speech_Therapists.csv
📄 TN_Occupational_Therapists.csv
Double-click either one — it opens in Excel, sorted by last name.

What to do with it (recruiting tips)

Filter to your cities first — in Excel, use the City column filter to show just the towns around your two locations.

Sort by the "Enumerated" column, newest first — recently-registered therapists are new grads and new arrivals to TN: the most recruitable people on the list.

Call, don't mass-email. The registry has phones, not emails (no public source has therapist emails — that's by design). For email reach, TAASLP and TNOTA rent their member lists for job posts.

If something goes wrong

The black window closing instantly, an error in red, or empty files — take a photo of the screen and send it to Claude. Nothing this runs can harm your computer; it only reads a public government website and writes two spreadsheets.
